About

One day, a powerful seraphim is sent to the world of mortals. Unable to get back into the heavens, he sets himself a goal to change the world to his own liking.

"Kaleido" is a fantasy adventure story, that touches the topics of love, friendship and communication, showing that everyone deserves to get a chance to change for the better.
